## 1. Getting Started

### How to Sign Up (New Customer)

1. Go to the website's home page.
2. Click the **"Sign Up"** button (usually at the top right or from the menu).
3. Enter your:
   - **Mobile Number** — your phone number (used to log in)
   - **Full Name**
   - **Email** (optional but recommended)
   - **Password**
4. You may receive an **OTP (One-Time Password)** on your phone to verify your number.
5. Once verified, your account is created and you are logged in.

### How to Log In (Returning Customer)

1. Click **"Login"** from the top menu.
2. Enter your registered **mobile number** and **password**.
3. Click **"Login"** — you will be taken back to the home page, now logged in.

> 💡 **Tip:** You must be logged in to add items to the cart, place orders, or view your order history.

## 3. Browsing & Searching

### Browsing by Category

1. Click on **"Categories"** from the top navigation or home page tiles.
2. You will see all available categories (e.g., Food, Electronics, Clothing).
3. Click a category to see all products in it.
4. On the category page, you can **filter** products by:
   - Sub-category
   - Price range
   - Brand
   - Rating
   - In-stock items only

### Browsing by Brand

1. Navigate to **"Brands"** from the menu.
2. Click on any brand logo to see all products from that brand.

### Searching for Products

1. Tap the **Search bar** at the top of any page.
2. Type what you're looking for (e.g., "atta", "phone case", "yoga mat").
3. The search uses **AI-powered smart search** — it understands what you mean even if you don't type the exact product name.
4. Results appear instantly as you type.
5. You can also filter and sort the search results.

> 💡 **Tip:** Searching "protein drink" will also show "whey protein", "health shake", etc. — the system understands related items.

### Flash Deals Page

- Click on any **Flash Deal banner** to see all current flash deals.
- These deals have a **countdown timer** — they expire at a set time.

## 4. Product Detail Page

When you click on any product, you go to its **Detail Page**. Here you can:

### What You See:
- **Product Images** — swipe/click to see all images and angles
- **Product Name & Brand**
- **Price** — shows the selling price and the original MRP (crossed out if discounted)
- **Discount Badge** — shows the % you're saving

- **"Buy Now" Button** — takes you straight to checkout
- **Wishlist Button (❤️)** — saves the product to your wishlist
- **Product Description** — detailed information about the product

- **Return Policy** — whether this product can be returned and within how many days

- **Similar Products** — other products you might like

### What is "Enquiry Only"?
Some products are marked as **Enquiry Only** — this means you cannot directly buy them online. Instead, a form appears where you can send your enquiry to the seller, who will contact you.

## 6. Checkout & Placing an Order

When you're ready to buy, go to **Checkout**. Here is what happens step by step:

### Step 1: Confirm Your Delivery Address
- Your saved addresses appear here.
- Select the address where you want the order delivered.
- Or click **"Add New Address"** to enter a new one.
- You can save multiple addresses (Home, Work, etc.).

### Step 2: Review Your Order Items
- All items from your cart are shown with quantities and prices.

### Step 3: Apply a Coupon (Optional)
- Click **"Apply Coupon"**.
- A panel slides up showing all available coupons.
- Select one and it's applied automatically.
- The discount is shown immediately.

### Step 4: Other Options
- **Gift Packaging** — toggle on if you want the order gift-wrapped (extra charge may apply).
- **GSTIN** — if you want a GST invoice for business purposes, enter your GSTIN number.
- **Add Tip** — you can optionally add a tip for the delivery person.
- **Order Notes** — add special instructions for the seller or delivery person.

### Step 5: Choose Payment Method
- **Razorpay (Online Payment)** — pay using UPI, credit card, debit card, net banking, wallets.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)** — pay in cash when the order is delivered.

### Step 6: Place the Order
- Click **"Place Order"** (for COD) or **"Pay Now"** (for online).
- For online payment, you'll be redirected to the Razorpay payment page.
- After successful payment, you see a **🎉 Order Success** screen with confetti.
- Your order number is shown, and you get a notification.

### Price Breakdown Shown at Checkout:
| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Subtotal | Total product cost before deductions |
| Coupon Discount | Amount saved with your coupon |
| Free Gift Discount | Value of any free gift unlocked |
| First Order Offer | Special discount for your very first order |
| Delivery Charge | Shipping cost (may be free above a minimum order) |
| Platform Fee | A small convenience fee |
| GST/Tax | Government tax on applicable products |
| Tip | Optional tip for delivery agent |
| **Total** | **Final amount you pay** |

## 7. Tracking Your Order

### Order Status Flow
After placing an order, it goes through these stages:

```
Received → Pending → Processed → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered
```

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Received** | Your order has been placed and the seller has received it |
| **Pending** | Seller is reviewing/preparing the order |
| **Processed** | Order is packed and ready |
| **Shipped** | Order is on its way from the seller |
| **Out for Delivery** | Delivery agent is on the way to your address |
| **Delivered** | Order successfully delivered |
| **Cancelled** | Order was cancelled (by you or the seller) |
| **Returned** | Order has been returned |

### Live Map Tracking
- Once the order is **Out for Delivery**, you can see the **delivery agent's live location** on a map.
- A **Delivery Partner Card** shows the agent's name and phone number.

### OTP-Verified Delivery
- When the delivery agent arrives, they will ask for a **delivery OTP**.
- This OTP is shown in your order detail page.
- Share this OTP with the agent to confirm delivery — this protects you from fake delivery confirmations.

### Viewing Your Orders
1. Click **"My Orders"** from the menu or account page.
2. See a list of all your past and current orders.
3. Click any order to see its **detailed status, items, and tracking**.

## 8. Returns & Replacements

If you're not satisfied with your order, you can request a **Return** or **Replacement**.

### How to Request a Return
1. Go to **"My Orders"** and click on the order.
2. Find the item you want to return.
3. Click **"Return Item"**.
4. Select a **reason** (damaged, wrong item, not as described, etc.).
5. Upload a **photo** of the item (required as proof).
6. Add any additional notes and submit.
7. The seller will review and respond.

### How to Request a Replacement
- Same process as return, but select **"Replacement"** instead.
- You'll receive a replacement item after the original is picked up.

### Important Notes:
- Returns are only available for products marked as **returnable**.
- There is a **maximum number of days** within which you can request a return (shown on the product page).
- The admin or seller will approve or reject your return request.

## 9. Wishlist

The **Wishlist** is your personal saved items list — products you like but aren't ready to buy yet.

### How to Use:

- Click the heart again to remove it.
- View your full wishlist from **My Account → Wishlist** or from the main menu.
- From the wishlist, you can add items directly to the cart.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to create an account to browse?**
A: No, you can browse products without logging in. But you need an account to add to cart, wishlist, and place orders.

**Q: Is my payment information safe?**
A: Yes. Payments are processed through Razorpay, a secure and trusted payment gateway. We never store your card details.

**Q: How do I know if a product is available in my area?**
A: The platform detects your location and shows only products available near you.

**Q: What if I receive a damaged product?**
A: Go to your order detail and request a return or replacement with a photo of the damage.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
A: Yes, you can cancel before the order is shipped. Go to order detail → Cancel Order. After shipping, contact support.
