## 1. Getting Started

### How to Sign Up as a Seller

1. Go to `/seller/signup` on the platform website.
2. Fill in your **Store Details:**
   - Store Name
   - Seller Name (your name)
   - Mobile Number
   - Email Address
   - Password
3. Fill in your **Business Details:**
   - Business Category
   - Store Address
   - City
   - Service Area Description
   - PAN Card Number (for tax purposes)
   - GSTIN (if applicable)
4. Upload required documents:
   - Profile/Logo Photo
   - ID Proof
   - Address Proof
5. Enter your **Bank Details** for receiving payments:
   - Account Holder Name
   - Bank Name
   - Branch
   - Account Number
   - IFSC Code
6. Set your **Location** on the map — this determines which customers can see your products.
7. Set your **Service Radius** (in km) — how far you're willing to deliver.
8. Submit the registration.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Your account will be in **"Pending"** status until the Admin approves it. You'll be notified once approved.

### How to Log In

1. Go to `/seller/login`.
2. Enter your registered **email** or **mobile number** and **password**.
3. Click **"Login"** — you'll be taken to your **Seller Dashboard**.

## 3. Managing Categories & Subcategories

As a seller, you manage your own product categories within the overall platform structure.

### Categories

**What is a Category?**
A category is a broad grouping of products (e.g., "Grocery", "Electronics", "Clothing").

**How to Add a Category:**
1. Go to **Categories** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **"Add Category"**.
3. Enter the category name.
4. Upload a category image (optional but recommended).
5. Click **"Save"**.

**How to Edit/Delete:**
- Each category has an **Edit** (pencil) and **Delete** (trash) icon.
- You cannot delete a category that has products in it.

### Subcategories

**What is a Subcategory?**
A subcategory is a more specific grouping under a category (e.g., under "Grocery" you might have "Rice & Pulses", "Snacks", "Beverages").

**How to Add a Subcategory:**
1. Go to **Subcategories** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"Add Subcategory"**.
3. Select the **parent category** this belongs to.
4. Enter the subcategory name.
5. Upload an image (optional).
6. Click **"Save"**.

## 4. Managing Products

This is where you list what you sell.

### How to Add a New Product

1. Go to **Products → Add Product** in the sidebar.
2. Fill in the **Basic Information:**
   - Product Name
   - Short Description (shown in listings)
   - Full Description (shown on product page)
   - Category & Subcategory
   - Brand (optional)

3. Set up **Variants/SKUs** (Every product must have at least one variant):
   - **Variation Type** — e.g., "Size", "Weight", "Color", "Standard"
   - **Value** — e.g., "500g", "1kg", "Red", "Standard"
   - **Retail Price (MRP)** — the original/maximum price
   - **Selling Price** — what customers pay
   - **Wholesale Price** — for wholesale/bulk buyers
   - **Purchase Price** — what you paid for it (for your records)
   - **Stock Quantity** — how many units you have
   - **SKU/Barcode** — unique identifier for the product

4. Add **Product Images:**
   - Main image (required)
   - Gallery images (multiple images showing different angles)

5. Set **Tax & Compliance:**
   - HSN Code (for GST)
   - GST Percentage
   - Tax Preference (Tax Included / Excluded / Hidden)

6. Set **Product Details (Optional):**
   - Manufacturer
   - Made In (country of origin)
   - Manufacturing Date
   - Expiry Date
   - FSSAI Licence Number (for food products)
   - Shelf Life
   - Pack size

7. Set **Return Policy:**
   - Is this product returnable? (Yes/No)
   - Maximum return days (e.g., 7, 15, 30 days)
   - Return policy text

8. Set **Availability:**
   - Delivery Time (e.g., "Same Day", "1-2 Days")
   - Total Allowed Quantity (max a customer can buy at once)
   - Low Stock Alert Threshold (notify when stock falls below this)

9. Set **SEO Details** (helps in search):
   - SEO Title
   - Keywords
   - Description

10. **Publish Settings:**
    - **Publish** — toggles whether the product is visible to customers.
    - **Popular** — marks it as a popular product.
    - **Deal of Day** — marks it as today's featured deal.

11. Click **"Save Product"**.

> ⚠️ **If Admin requires product approval:** Your product will show as "Pending" until the Admin reviews and approves it.

### How to Edit a Product
1. Go to **Products → Product List**.
2. Find the product, click the **Edit** icon.
3. Make your changes and click **"Save"**.

### How to Delete a Product
- Click the **Delete** icon on the product list.
- Confirm the deletion.

### Product List View
The **Product List** shows all your products in a table with:
- Product image & name
- Category
- Price & stock
- Status (Active/Inactive/Pending)
- Quick actions (edit, delete, toggle publish)

## 5. Stock Management

**Stock Management** lets you see and update the quantity (stock) of all your products.

### Viewing Stock
1. Go to **Products → Stock Management**.
2. See a list of all products with their current stock quantities.
3. Filter by category, subcategory, or low-stock status.

### Updating Stock
- Click the **edit** button next to a product.
- Enter the new stock quantity.
- Save.

### Stock Alerts
- Products below the **low stock threshold** are highlighted.
- Out-of-stock products are marked clearly.

### Bulk Stock Update (Import)
- Go to **Products → Bulk Stock Import**.
- Download the Excel template.
- Fill in the stock quantities in the template.
- Upload the file to update stock for many products at once.

### Bulk Edit
- Go to **Products → Bulk Edit**.
- Edit prices, stock, and other fields for multiple products in a spreadsheet-like view.

### Storage Location
- Each product can be assigned a **storage location** (City → Warehouse → Room → Rack Number).
- This helps staff quickly locate products in your physical store.

### Barcode Settings
- Generate and print barcodes for your products.
- You can scan barcodes during POS billing to quickly add products.

## 6. Online Order Management

When customers order online, you manage everything from the **Orders** section.

### Order Status Flow

```
Received → Pending → Processed → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered
```

### Viewing Orders

Go to **Orders** in the sidebar. You can filter by status:
- **All Orders** — every order
- **Pending** — new orders waiting for action
- **Received** — orders acknowledged
- **Processed** — packed and ready
- **Shipped** — handed to courier/delivery
- **Out for Delivery** — with delivery agent
- **Delivered** — successfully delivered
- **Cancelled** — cancelled orders

### Processing an Order (Step-by-Step)

1. **New Order Arrives** → You receive a notification. Status is "Received".
2. **Review the Order** → Click on the order to see items, customer address, payment status.
3. **Accept/Process** → Click **"Mark as Processed"** when you've packed it.
4. **Assign Delivery** → If you use the platform's delivery, a delivery agent will be assigned automatically. Or mark it as "Shipped" with a tracking number for third-party courier.
5. **Out for Delivery** → Agent picks up the order and status updates.
6. **Delivered** → Agent confirms delivery with OTP.

### Order Detail View

When you click on any order, you see:
- Customer name, phone, delivery address
- List of all items ordered with quantities and prices
- Order total, payment method, payment status
- Order status timeline
- Options to update status, add notes, contact customer

### Return & Replacement Requests

- Go to **Return Requests** or **Replace Requests**.
- See all customer requests with their reason and photos.
- Accept or reject the request with a message to the customer.

## 7. POS (In-Store Billing)

The **POS (Point of Sale)** system is a full in-store billing terminal for selling to walk-in customers.

> For a detailed POS guide, see: **05-POS-BILLING-SYSTEM.md**

### Quick Overview:

**What is POS?**
POS lets you create bills for customers who come to your physical store — no online ordering needed. It works like a cash register.

**How to Open POS:**
1. Go to **POS → Orders** in the sidebar.
2. The POS screen opens with:
   - Product search/selection on the left
   - Current bill/cart on the right

**Key POS Features:**
- Search products by name or **scan barcode**
- Select product variants
- Choose Retail or Wholesale pricing
- Apply discounts at item or bill level
- Multiple payment methods (Cash, Card, UPI, Credit)
- Create multiple bills at once (Bill 1, Bill 2, etc.)
- Generate and print bill receipt
- Save bills as **Quotations**
- Customer search and management
- Supplier management and purchase entries

## 8. Seller Wallet & Earnings

Your **Wallet** tracks all your earnings from orders on the platform.

### Wallet Overview

Go to **Wallet** in the sidebar to see:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Available Balance** | Money you can withdraw right now |
| **Total Earnings** | All-time earnings from orders |
| **Pending Settlement** | Earnings not yet released to your wallet |
| **Total Withdrawn** | Amount you've already withdrawn |

### Tabs in Wallet:

**1. Transactions Tab**
- Complete history of every credit and debit in your wallet.
- Filter by date range or status.

**2. Withdrawals Tab**
- See all your withdrawal requests and their status (Pending/Approved/Rejected/Completed).

**3. Earnings Tab**
- See earnings broken down by order.
- Shows gross amount, commission deducted, and net earning per order.

### How to Request a Withdrawal

1. Click **"Withdraw"** button.
2. Enter the **amount** you want to withdraw.
3. Choose payment method:
   - **Bank Transfer** — enter account details
   - **UPI** — enter your UPI ID
4. Click **"Submit Request"**.
5. The Admin will review and approve/process your withdrawal.
6. Once approved, money is transferred to your account.

> 💡 **Note:** The platform deducts a **commission percentage** from each order before crediting to your wallet. The commission rate is set by the Admin.

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: What happens if I run out of stock?**
A: The product is automatically marked as "Out of Stock" and customers cannot add it to cart. Restock to make it available again.

**Q: Can I offer different prices for wholesale buyers?**
A: Yes. Every product variant has a separate **Wholesale Price**. When using POS, you can switch between Retail and Wholesale modes.

**Q: What is the difference between a Return and a Replace request?**
A: A Return means the customer wants their money back. A Replace means the customer wants the same product again (maybe it was defective).

**Q: How long does it take to receive my earnings?**
A: Earnings from delivered orders are credited to your wallet after the settlement period set by the Admin. You can then withdraw at any time.

**Q: What is "Commission"?**
A: The Admin charges a percentage of each sale as a platform fee (commission). For example, if commission is 10% and you sold ₹1,000, you get ₹900 in your wallet.
