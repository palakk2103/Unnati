## 1. Getting Started

### How to Sign Up as a Delivery Agent

1. Go to `/delivery/signup` on the platform.
2. Fill in your **Personal Details:**
   - Full Name
   - Mobile Number
   - Email
   - Password
3. Fill in your **Work Details:**
   - Vehicle Type (bike, scooter, bicycle, etc.)
   - Vehicle Number
4. Upload required documents:
   - Profile Photo
   - ID Proof (Aadhar, Driving Licence, etc.)
5. Set your **location** on the map.
6. Submit the form.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Your account needs **Admin approval** before you can start accepting deliveries. You'll receive a notification once approved.

### How to Log In

1. Go to `/delivery/login`.
2. Enter your **mobile number** and **password**.
3. Click **"Login"** — you're taken to your **Dashboard**.

## 2. The Dashboard

The **Delivery Dashboard** is your main screen. It shows everything you need to know for the day.

### Key Information on Dashboard:

| Section | What It Shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Daily Collection** | Total cash you've collected today |
| **Cash Balance** | Total cash currently in your hands (to be submitted) |
| **Today's Pending Orders** | How many orders are waiting for delivery today |
| **Today's All Orders** | Total orders assigned to you today |
| **Return & Replace Tasks** | Return orders you need to pick up |
| **Total Return Items** | Count of items you're currently holding for returns |
| **Today's Earning** | Money earned today from deliveries |
| **Total Earning** | All-time total earnings |

### Active Service Areas Card

This shows:
- A **pulsing green dot** when you are **Online** and in a seller's delivery zone.
- How many seller zones you are currently in.
- Click this card to see the **Sellers in Range** screen.

### Pending Orders List

Below the cards, you see a list of **today's pending orders** with:
- Order ID
- Customer name
- Delivery address
- Total amount
- Order status
- Estimated delivery time (if set)

Click any order to go to its details.

## 4. Delivering an Order — Step by Step

Here is the complete step-by-step process for delivering an order:

### Step 1: Receive Order Assignment
- When Admin assigns an order to you, you receive a **push notification**.
- The order appears in your **Pending Orders** list.

### Step 2: Open Order Details
- Tap on the order to open the **Order Detail** screen.
- You'll see:
  - Customer's name and phone number
  - Complete delivery address
  - List of items in the order
  - Total amount (to collect if Cash on Delivery)
  - Payment method (Online / Cash on Delivery)
  - Any special delivery instructions

### Step 3: Go to the Seller (for Pickup)
- Get the order from the seller's store.
- The seller's address is shown on the map (if available).
- You can call the seller directly from the app.

### Step 4: Pick Up the Order
- Physically collect the packaged order from the seller.
- Update the status to **"Picked Up"** in the app.

### Step 5: Head to Customer's Address
- The delivery address is shown clearly.
- Tap **"Navigate"** to open Google Maps with directions to the customer.
- Your live location is tracked and shared with the customer through the app.

### Step 6: Arrive at Customer's Location
- Call the customer if needed (their phone number is shown).
- The customer can track your live location in their app.

### Step 7: Collect Payment (If Cash on Delivery)
- If the payment method is **Cash on Delivery (COD)**, collect the exact amount from the customer.
- The amount is clearly shown on the order detail screen.

### Step 8: Get the Delivery OTP
- Ask the customer for their **Delivery OTP** (One-Time Password).
- This OTP is shown to the customer in their order detail page.
- Enter the OTP in your app to confirm the delivery.
- This protects both you and the customer — only a real delivery can be confirmed this way.

### Step 9: Mark as Delivered
- After entering the correct OTP, the order status updates to **"Delivered"**.
- The delivery is complete!
- Your earnings for this delivery are added to your wallet.

## 5. Handling Return Orders

Sometimes customers want to **return** or **replace** an item. You may be assigned to pick up such items.

### Return Order Process:

1. You receive a **Return Task** notification.
2. Go to **Orders → Return Orders** to see it.
3. Open the return order details:
   - Customer name and address
   - Item to be picked up
   - Reason for return
   - Photos provided by customer
4. Go to the customer's address.
5. Collect the return item from the customer.
6. Bring it back to the seller.
7. Mark the return as **"Collected"** in the app.

> 💡 **Note:** Return items are tracked separately. Your "Total Return Items" count on the dashboard shows how many items you currently have with you.

## 6. Earnings & Cash Collection

### How You Earn

You earn a **per-delivery fee** for each successful delivery. The amount is set by the Admin and may vary based on:
- Distance
- Order type (regular vs. express)
- Time of day

### Earnings Screen

Go to **Menu → Earnings** to see:
- Your **today's earnings**
- **Total all-time earnings**
- Earnings history by date

### Cash Collection

- For **Cash on Delivery** orders, you collect cash from customers.
- This cash accumulates as your **"Cash Balance"** shown on the dashboard.
- You must **submit/deposit** this cash to the Admin or designated collection point regularly.
- After submitting, the Admin updates the **Fund Transfer** in the system.

### Cash Balance Dashboard

- **Daily Collection** = Cash collected today from deliveries.
- **Cash Balance** = Total cash in your hand not yet submitted.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Always submit your collected cash regularly as instructed by your supervisor/Admin.

## 7. Your Profile

Go to **Menu → Profile** to view and manage your information.

### What You Can View/Edit:
- Profile photo
- Full name
- Mobile number
- Email
- Vehicle type and number
- Current status (Online/Offline)
- Documents uploaded

### Going Online / Offline

- From your profile or menu, you can toggle yourself **Online** or **Offline**.
- When **Online**, you show up as available in seller zones.
- When **Offline**, you won't receive new order assignments.

> 💡 **Always go Online when you start your shift and Offline when you finish.**

## 9. Sellers in My Range

This feature shows you which **seller stores are within your current location's service radius**.

### How to Use:

1. Make sure you are **Online** (go to Profile and toggle Online).
2. From the Dashboard, click the **Active Service Areas** card.
3. Or go to **Menu → Sellers in Range**.
4. A list/map appears showing all sellers whose delivery zone you're currently in.

### Why is This Useful?
- Know which sellers you can pick up orders from.
- Helps you plan your delivery route efficiently.
- If you're not in any seller's range, you won't receive orders from that seller.

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: What do I do if the customer is not available at the address?**
A: Call the customer using the phone number in the order details. If unreachable, contact your Admin for instructions.

**Q: What if the customer refuses to give the OTP?**
A: You cannot confirm delivery without an OTP. Contact your Admin for guidance.

**Q: What happens if I accidentally mark a wrong status?**
A: Contact your Admin immediately to correct the order status.

**Q: Can I reject/decline an order assignment?**
A: Contact your Admin if you cannot fulfill an assigned order. Do not ignore assignments.

**Q: What do I do if the item I'm delivering is damaged?**
A: Do not deliver a visibly damaged item. Contact your Admin immediately with photos.

**Q: How long before I get my earnings?**
A: Earnings from delivered orders are credited to your account as per the Admin's payment cycle. Check the Earnings screen for details.
